構造Structure

Four places,
one path.

The four names stay: Learn, Browse, My list and Teach. What changes is what they own. Today the footer lists twelve destinations, and theme and radical pages light up “Learn” while kanji pages light up “Browse”. Below, every route belongs to exactly one of the four, and every atlas page shows where it sits on the same path.

四処

Navigation

Same four on the desktop header and the phone tab bar

学Learn

Read the atlas: start from a theme, open a radical, land on a kanji. The home page is its contents page.

  • /
  • /category/[theme]
  • /radical/[key]
  • /kanji/[char] · #learn
  • /categories · /radicals (index)

探Browse

Find kanji by what you know about them: JLPT, strokes, theme, radical, reading, ink. One filter panel, one tile grid.

  • /browse?jlpt=&theme=…
  • /search?q= (same grid)

墨My list

Your ink: what you've saved, how dark each kanji is, and the decks you can study. Study is optional and nothing comes due.

  • /my-list
  • /study?chars= (deck)
  • /practice (classic path, linked here only)

教Teach

Build a set, then print it, project it or share a link. Paper output is always 墨.

  • /teach
  • /teach/print · /teach/present
  • /set?k=&t= (student link)

Footer only: About & credits, Settings, Feedback, GitHub. The extra links to Themes, Radicals, Study and Practice go away; they are reached from Learn and My list. The feedback card shrinks to a single line in the footer.

  1. 1Meaning + partsIn the reading panel, before anything scrolls
  2. 2Readings with wordsEach reading next to the words that use it
  3. 3Learn · SaveOne primary action. Card, print and present go in a “More” row
  4. 4Origin · storyFact and mnemonic side by side, never mixed
  5. 5ConnectionsSame part, same sound, look-alikes, all as tiles
