Where it comes from

Origin · sourced

Ideogrammic compound 会意人 man + 木 tree

A man leaning against a tree, resting.

Source: Wiktionary, 休 (rev. 91675236), CC BY-SA 4.0

Memory story · made up to help you remember

“A person leans against a tree and takes a break from the sun.”

Uses the parts above. Not a claim about history.
