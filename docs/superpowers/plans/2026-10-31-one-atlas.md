# One Atlas Implementation Plan (round 6)

> **For agentic workers:** The main session runs this plan. Phase 1 is done in the main session, because every page depends on it. Phase 2 hands the pages to subagents, each in its own git worktree, with no files shared between them. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax. Tick a box in the commit that completes it. Read "Contracts" and "Working method" before you start a phase.

**Goal:** Make v2.1.0 (branch `claude/story-atlas-847c2a`) read as one product. Every page is drawn from one small system. Five rules decide every colour, line, fill and glow. A kanji, a radical and a theme each have exactly one visual form. Atlas pages share one layout ("Plate") and tool pages share another ("Tool"). Every page is reached from the same four places, which keep their current names: Learn, Browse, My list and Teach. What changes is which routes each one owns.

**Why:** The owner judged v2.1.0's overall coherence "very poor" (2026-10-31). It was built by eight parallel workstreams, and each one invented its own layout and vocabulary. Counted in `app/` and `components/` on this branch:

- **Seven page layouts.** Centred hero plus bento (home), full-bleed art plus a floating panel (theme), two-column player (kanji, radical), filter box (browse), card grid (study), form tool (teach) and a plain document (about).
- **A kanji in a list is drawn at least six ways:**
  - `KanjiTile` (with a JLPT-coloured bottom border)
  - `cellClass` cells (theme page, radical page)
  - the "Also built on" lists in `KanjiLinks`
  - look-alike pills
  - the kanji-of-the-day card
  - the ink-dot strip in the kanji hero
- **A radical is drawn four ways:** `sealClass` seals in five states, the `RadicalSealGrid` with its 古 mark, `PartChips` coloured by theme (so 休 shows a blue 亻 and a green 木), and `RadicalChip`.
- **Progress is shown six ways:** `InkRing` (%), the `ThemeScroll` card, `InkDots`, `InkBar` on gates, "you know N" counts and JLPT-coloured bars.
- **Colour has no rule:**
  - The mint `--accent` (#3BE0A6) appears in 48 files (144 uses).
  - JLPT colours appear in 7 files. N5 green collides with Nature, and N1 red with Action.
  - `glow`/`halo` appear in 16 files, on headings and buttons as well as strokes.
  - `italic` appears in 15 files, so mnemonic and fact look the same.
- **Navigation contradicts itself.** The header has 4 items and the footer 12. Theme and radical pages mark "Learn" as current, while kanji pages mark "Browse".

## Product decisions

### Kept from the story-atlas plan (do not reopen)

No hearts, streaks, lock-outs or due counts. Ink has five shades and never drops after a wrong answer. Themes have no order. Etymology is shown only with a source. Ancient forms are never drawn by hand. 夜 is the default skin and worksheets always print in 墨. Fonts are Shippori Mincho B1, Cormorant Garamond and Zen Kaku Gothic New.

### New in this plan (approved with the design by the owner, 2026-10-31)

1. **Nav labels and URLs stay as they are** (owner, 2026-10-31): Learn, Browse, My list, Teach. Renaming them for the redesign's sake adds nothing. Only route ownership changes: kanji pages belong to Learn (with themes and radicals), not Browse. The boards were relabelled to match.
2. **Colour = theme.** The theme colour marks identity and progress only. Primary buttons are ink (light on dark in 夜, dark on paper in 墨). The mint accent and all JLPT colours are removed, and JLPT levels become plain tags.
3. **Stroke order appears once**, as the Strokes tab of the kanji specimen. The second, static numbered diagram goes away (the tab shows the numbers).
4. **Footer is one row of links.** The big feedback card becomes a "Send feedback" link. The personal site and email move to `/about`.
5. **Phone:** a bottom tab bar (学 Learn · 探 Browse · 墨 My list · 教 Teach) replaces the hamburger menu. Settings stays an icon in the top bar. Phone kanji pages open on Readings · Strokes · Ancient, because the parts already sit under the title.
6. **Removed from home:** the featured-theme deck with its ring and the kanji of the day. **Removed from the theme page:** the long "All kanji by radical" list. In its place are JLPT chips that open Browse filtered to the theme. Radical pages still link every kanji, so no kanji page loses its inbound link.
7. **A seal's fill is the rounded mean shade of the radical's 8 most common kanji.** It uses the same five fills as kanji. This replaces the five `SealState`s, including the pulsing "now" seal. The "next" radical is marked by the selected ring instead.
8. **Contrast fix:** in 夜, the 濃 fill mixes 45% theme colour into the card instead of the canvas's 60%. At 60%, 12 px meaning text fell below 4.5:1 for Nature, Home, Animals, Tools, Plants and Time (lowest 3.47). At 45% the lowest is 5.40. Run `node scripts/check-ink-contrast.mjs` to reproduce.

## Contracts (every workstream codes against these)

### The five rules (enforced by the grep gates in 3.1)

1. **Colour = theme.** Only `--c` and `--sc` carry colour. They resolve from the nearest `--tc` (set with `themeVars()`) in 夜 and to vermilion or black ink in 墨. With no theme, `--c` is `--ink`.
2. **Line = role.** A part that carries meaning gets a solid accent line. A sound part gets a dashed accent line. A form-only part gets a grey line. This holds on every page, the share card included.
3. **Fill = your ink.** A kanji's shade shows only as its tile fill (`ink-0` … `ink-4`). A radical shows the same fills. Groups (themes, radicals, decks) show one `PaintedBar`. Nothing else shows progress.
4. **Italic = story.** Only `MemoryStory` and the share card's memory line are italic. Sourced etymology is roman and always shows its source.
5. **Glow = live.** Only a stroke being drawn and the current learn step glow (`live`, 夜 only). Headings, buttons, seals and cards never glow.

### Tokens (`app/globals.css`)

Values come from `docs/design/one-atlas.css`. Keep the existing skin mechanism (`data-skin`, the switch variables, `ink-art`, `scroll-base`).

- `--accent` becomes `var(--ink)` in both skins in step 1.1, which neutralises its 144 uses at once. Phase 2 then replaces the remaining uses with `ink`/`c` utilities, and 3.1 deletes the token.
- Delete `--n1`…`--n5`, `--color-n*`, `jlptColor()` and the JLPT styling in `JlptBadge`.
- Add the following:
  - `--raise`
  - `--on3` (text on a 濃 fill: `--ink` in 夜, `--card` in 墨)
  - `--paper`
  - `--i3` (45% 夜 / 60% 墨)
  - `--skin-sc` (`initial` in 夜, `#1B1915` in 墨)
  - `--sc`, declared beside `--c` in the `*` rule as `var(--skin-sc, var(--tc, var(--ink)))`
- **Shade utilities** `ink-0`…`ink-4`, plus the same styles on `[data-ink="0"]`…`[data-ink="4"]` (see "Ink painting"):
  - 0: dashed `--line-2`, transparent, `--ink-3` text
  - 1: 9% `--sc` on card
  - 2: 24%
  - 3: `--i3`, `--on3` text
  - 4: `--c` fill, `--onacc` text
- **`live`** replaces `glow` and `halo`. It applies `drop-shadow` in `--c` in 夜 and nothing in 墨. Put it on the `<svg>` that holds the live stroke, not on a `<path>`.
- **Type utilities** (the only display styles):
  - `type-title`: Cormorant 700, 56 px, 40 px under `sm`
  - `type-section`: Cormorant 600, 30 / 24 px
  - `type-label`: Zen Kaku 700, 12 px, caps, `.12em`, `--ink-3`
  - `type-story`: Cormorant italic, 21 px
  - Body text stays on Tailwind sizes (16 / 14 / 12).
- **Shapes:** tile `rounded-xl` (12), card `rounded-2xl` (16), pill `rounded-full`, seal circle, gate arch (`999px 999px 14px 14px`). There are no shadows except `live`.

### Ink painting (one client island per page)

Tiles and seals stay server-rendered, so a 600-tile theme page doesn't become 600 client components. Each page wraps its atlas content in one `<InkPaint>` (client, `components/Ink/InkPaint.tsx`). After `useInkHydrated()`, and on every store change, it does two things:

- sets `data-ink` on each `[data-kanji]` descendant to that kanji's shade;
- sets `data-ink` on each `[data-radical-chars]` descendant to `sealShade(chars)`.

The server output is `data-ink="0"`, which matches today's "all not met until hydration" behaviour. The pure helpers go in `lib/ink-paint.ts` with tests:

- `sealShade(shades)` returns the rounded mean of the first 8 shades;
- `paintedShare(shades)` returns Σ/(4·N), reusing `themeProgress`.
